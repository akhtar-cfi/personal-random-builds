1. **Project Rakshak** (crashfreeindia.org/our-work/project-rakshak — "Project Rakshak — Youth-Led Road Safety")
   - "Youth-led mission auditing high-risk junctions and driving infrastructural change across Indian cities… audits, advocacy and on-ground action."
   - 3-month Infrastructure Internship by Crashfree India (a CARS24 initiative) + Indian Road Safety Council: students identify unsafe blackspots, work with traffic police & civic bodies, propose design fixes. Cohorts pick a high-risk corridor and work it end-to-end (data → audits → advocacy → fixes).
   - NUMBERS (from CFI's Rakshak report / LinkedIn content, surfaced in search — best proxy for dashboard): **31 teams, 18 cities, 900+ stakeholder surveys, 23+ authority approvals, 20–30% crash-reduction potential**. Report documents city-wise findings w/ photos and case for national scaling.
   - Issues targeted: overspeeding/unsafe interactions at uncontrolled intersections; non-functional or absent signals/signage; missing safe pedestrian/cyclist crossings; potholes, uneven surfaces, water-logging.
   - Event: "Project Rakshak National Road Safety Forum" hosted at IIT Delhi (CFI LinkedIn post "exactly one month since we hosted…", activity id ~7460211703809617920, i.e. forum ~Dec 2025/Jan 2026; YouTube short exists). ### Piyush Tewari (spelling: TEWARI — not Tiwari)
- Founder & CEO, SaveLIFE Foundation (SLF), founded 2008 after his 16-year-old cousin died in a 2007 crash in Kanpur for want of bystander help/timely care.
- b. 19 May 1980, Kanpur; B. in IT, Delhi University; MPA, Harvard University.
- **Good Samaritan Law**: SLF's PIL led to Supreme Court of India making Good Samaritan protection guidelines binding on 30 March 2016; later codified (Motor Vehicles Amendment Act 2019 — from general knowledge, verify).
- **Zero Fatality Corridor (ZFC)**: SLF model redesigning dangerous highways using data, engineering, enforcement, emergency-care reform; famously cut deaths on Mumbai-Pune Expressway (WEF story: reduced fatalities ~40%+ — WEF May 2024 piece: "one man's determination has reduced that figure by 40%"). Since 2021, partnership with MoRTH scaling ZFC to top 100 most dangerous highways.
- Awards/fellowships: Rolex Award for Enterprise (2010), Elevate Prize (2023), Ashoka Fellow, Echoing Green Fellow, Rainer Arnhold Fellow, WEF Young Global Leader, DRK Foundation Entrepreneur. Named to India's National Road Safety Council (2021). Advisor to IIT Kharagpur's School of Infrastructure Design & Management.
- Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piyush_Tewari; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SaveLIFE_Foundation; savelifefoundation.org/stuff/people-piyush-tewari/; weforum.org/people/piyush-tewari/; skoll.org/organization/savelife-foundation/.

### Prof. Geetam Tiwari (spelling: TIWARI — not Tewari)
- Long-time TRIPP Chair Professor, Dept of Civil Engineering, IIT Delhi; teaching at IIT Delhi since 1990. **Current listing on tripc.iitd.ac.in says "Emeritus Professor"** — title has likely transitioned from TRIPP Chair Professor to Emeritus Professor at TRIP Centre (TRIPC), IIT Delhi. USE "Emeritus Professor, Transportation Research and Injury Prevention Centre (TRIPC), IIT Delhi" and verify.
- Education: B.Arch, University of Roorkee (1980); Masters in Transport Planning & Policy and PhD in Public Policy Analysis (Transport Planning), University of Illinois at Chicago.
- Research: transport planning/safety for low-income countries, vulnerable road users, non-motorised transport, bus systems/BRT, pedestrian safety.
- TRIPP/TRIPC is a **WHO Collaborating Centre for Research and Training in Safety Technology** (tripc.iitd.ac.in/about/recognition).
- Honors: IRTE & Prince Michael International Road Safety Award (2002); Honorary Doctorate, Chalmers University (2012).
- Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geetam_Tiwari; tripc.iitd.ac.in/people/faculty_details/geetam-tiwari-phd-uni-of-illinois-chicago-emeritus-professor; tripc.iitd.ac.in/about/recognition.
