## Canonical numbers (single source of truth for this deck)
- Deaths: 1,77,175 killed on Indian roads in 2024 (MoRTH RAI, via CFI Crash Data Dashboard) = 485/day = ~1 every 3 minutes
- 59% of road fatalities involve no traffic violation (MoRTH) — the alibi-killer
- 66% of victims aged 18–34 (2024, Dhoni PR); India ~1% of world's vehicles, ~11% of its road deaths
- Rakshak funnel: 120+ locations screened → 31 sites audited (11 official blackspots) → 25+ written government approvals → 15 implementations begun · 18 cities · 20 institutions incl. 9 IITs · 900+ stakeholder surveys · live public dashboard
- Justice: ₹80,000 Cr stuck across 10.46 lakh pending MACT cases · hit-and-run FY22-23: 205 claims filed vs ~25,000 eligible crashes · Gurugram audit: 102 files since 2022, 2 settled · avg 3.6-yr timeline · 70% low-income families unaware
- SATARK: 3,00,000+ vehicles scanned · ₹30 Cr+ pending challans surfaced · 350+ intercepted · live Jaipur + Bengaluru · 5 police approvals · first 3 days Jaipur: 1,240 flagged, 58 intercepted · 100 AI challan boards live Gurugram
- Aasha: India's first multilingual 24/7 AI compensation chatbot + Calculator (Sarla Verma / Pranay Sethi principles) + hospital helpdesk: 100+ victims supported in 4 days
- Data commons: 4 self-updating public goods — Defect Repository (146 districts, 13 languages) · Parliament tracker (1,423 questions; 282 LS MPs never asked one) · SC Litigation Tracker (18 cases, 190+ orders, 11 deadlines overdue at launch) · Crash Data Dashboard (36 states + 50 cities, CC-BY)
- Org: launched June 2025 · Cars24 commitment $3M/3yr · Vision Zero Trust · 150+ members · 50+ experts · 4 DRSCs embedded · MoUs Jaipur Traffic Police, Gurugram Police · 1M+ citizens reached · 10+ national media features (Deccan Herald, ET, Fortune India, IndiaSpend, Moneycontrol, Scroll.in, The Wire)
- Year-2 (AOP, ALL TARGETS): 150+ blackspots · 100+ recommendations accepted · 12+ DRSC presentations · model districts Gurugram / SW Delhi / Jaipur · Rakshak Audit Manual v1.0 · Expert Consortium
- NOT featured as forward bets: School Safety Index, Gig Rider Safety (per owner instruction)
