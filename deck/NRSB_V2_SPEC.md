## DESIGN SYSTEM v2 (overrides CFI skill defaults — user authorized)
- Paper white bg; INK #16181D titles (Montserrat SemiBold ~23pt, full literal sentences, max 2 lines, one word/phrase in BRAND).
- BRAND #4A35FF only for: key numbers, 1-2 keywords per title, small chips. NO blue kickers, NO lavender panels as default.
- Neutral: MIST #F7F7FA card fill, LINE #E8E9EE hairlines, MUT #6E7180 gray text.
- Accent tints: GREEN #0A9955 (wins/approvals), AMBER #E58900 (gaps/frictions), RED #E11900 (max 1-2 uses/deck), TEAL #0E8A8A occasional.
- Numbered section rail like SATARK one-pager: "01 · ABOUT US" small tracking caps + hairline.
- Running agenda: top-right chip on every content slide "SECTION n / 5 · <NAME>".
- Icon style: lucide in tinted circles (0.4"), icon color = section accent. Use generously (every bullet row).
- Photos on nearly every slide: rect, 1px LINE border, caption 8.5pt gray below.
- Footer: hairline + "Crashfree India · Expression of Interest — Working Group N" left, "p / N" right.
- Cover: white, big title, thin brand rule, 4 stat chips, bottom photo filmstrip (4 photos), no Dhoni on cover.
- Close: keep one brand-blue slide (only major blue moment) with offer recap + contacts.

### WG-2 (Safe Roads, Speeds & VRUs) S13-S21
S13 What is Project Rakshak (explainer!) — one-sentence definition: "Project Rakshak is our flagship programme where trained
   student teams, guided by senior road-safety experts, audit dangerous road locations and CFI walks each fix through the
   road-owning authority until it is built." Who-does-what 3 cards (Students audit · Experts verify · Authorities build, CFI tracks)
   + 4-phase timeline + audit photo.
S14 Why it fits WG-2 — identification has outpaced rectification: 13,795 identified vs 5,036 permanently rectified
   [Parliamentary Standing Committee 2024]; 44 certified RSAs on national registry; low-cost measures cut crashes 20–80% (iRAP/PIARC).
   Trained, supervised auditing capacity = the constraint Rakshak addresses WITH authorities.
S15 Year-one footprint — funnel 120+ screened → 31 audited (11 official blackspots) → 25+ written approvals → 15 implementations
   underway; 18 cities; 20 institutions incl. 9 IITs; 900+ stakeholder surveys; India map; est. 20–50% crash reduction at treated
   sites (to be verified w/ before-after data).
S16 Approvals in writing — letter wall (6 letters) + authority list: PWD Kerala, PWD UP, PWD Punjab, CPWD + Addl. Collector
   Indore, ADDA Durgapur, R&B Andhra Pradesh, GCC Chennai.
S17 On the ground — intervention photo grid (parking, drainage, markings, signage, footpaths, barricading) + Tejaji Nagar Indore
   case (official blackspot, CPWD-approved redesign) + Guntur junction photo.
S18 Implementation dashboard — founder-deck dashboard page crop + Rakshak portal screenshot; every site tracked
   submission→completion; PRAGATI parallel (7,000+ flagged issues, ₹86 lakh crore projects reviewed) — same principle,
   visible follow-up.
S19 Standards & method — every audit anchored to IRC codes + MoRTH checklists; guided by Prof. Geetam Tiwari (IRC H-7) &
   Dr. Richa Ahuja (IIT KGP); masterclasses (Prof. Tiwari Safe System, Dr. Velmurugan on IRC-131/SP-55); every recommendation

### WG-5 (Post-Crash Care, Policy Standards & State Implementation) S13-S21
S13 The victim's journey — 7-stage strip FIR→Case flagged→Family traced→File assembled→Verified→Sanctioned→Paid (real Jaipur
   mapping) + where CFI supports each stage. "A family meets many institutions; our work makes each hand-off visible."
S14 Justice Unserved evidence — report cover + 5 gaps (Awareness 70% low-income unaware · Timeliness 3.6-yr avg, 8–10-yr cases ·
   Documentation 90% H&R claims stall on documents · Adequacy informal workers valued at min wage · Equity 14% low-income vs
   24% high-income compensated) + headline 205 hit-and-run claims filed FY22-23 vs ~25,000 eligible. Frame: scheme is sound,
   pipeline needs support.
S15 District machinery — 4 DRSCs joined (Gurugram, SW Delhi, NW Delhi, Jaipur; MoUs Jaipur Traffic Police & Gurugram Police);
   102 hit-and-run files reviewed w/ Gurugram admin; SW Delhi DRSC minutes task CFI w/ digitisation+analytics; research fellow
   embedded; helpdesk photos.
S16 Rajasthan working template — Jaipur ~180 of ~395 monthly H&R claims (~46%); what works: outreach + single coordination cell;
   frictions: 11–13 docs asked vs 7 court-agreed standard, ~50% families unreachable; 5 GIC clarification queries drafted;

S17 The portal concept — WhatsApp family flow phone mockups + officer verification-queue UI + AI pre-check UI; 5 design

   clarification · evidence for administrators); 8-state case machine; Phase 1 = traceability.
S18 Aasha & tools — chatbot detail (24/7, multilingual, WhatsApp+voice, guides FIR→DLSA→documents) + Calculator (SC principles,

   workshops ~400 people.
S19 Helpdesks + EMS evidence — hospital legal helpdesk (100+ victims in 4 days; photos of camps w/ law students, DLSA) +

S20 The pipeline, measured — stat tiles 921 of 1,026 claims stalled at documentation (Jul 2024) · ~60 days intake latency ·
