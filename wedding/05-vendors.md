## Checklist for vetting any vendor
- [ ] Written quote with **inclusions/exclusions** spelled out
- [ ] Advance vs balance schedule + **cancellation/refund** terms
- [ ] Availability confirmed **for your exact date(s)**
- [ ] Reviews / past work seen (real samples, not just portfolio)
- [ ] Who is the actual person on the day (esp. photographer/makeup)
- [ ] Overtime / extra-hour charges known upfront
