# Crashfree India — Team Profile Research Notes
Research date: 2026-07-28. Purpose: enrich one-line bios for investor deck.
Note: crashfreeindia.org, thewire.in, indiaspend.com, scroll.in, rocketreach.co and openthemagazine.com all returned 403 to direct fetch from this environment; facts below come from search-index snippets of those pages plus accessible sources. Confidence levels reflect that.

## Org-level context (useful for deck)
- Crashfree India (CFI) launched by CARS24 as a dedicated nonprofit; mission: zero road-crash fatalities by 2040. Implemented by Vision Zero Trust; advised by Amar Srivastava and Deepanshu Gupta, co-founders of the Indian Road Safety Council (IRSC). (Team-BHP, CSR Universe, Adgully coverage.)
- Five focus areas: data infrastructure, policy reform, road design, technology, behavioural change.
- M.S. Dhoni joined CFI as Goodwill Ambassador (~April 2026; referenced in team LinkedIn post of 14 Apr 2026).
- Published research brief "Justice Unserved" (January 2026), crashfreeindia.org/documents/justice-unserved-crashfree-india.pdf.
- Runs Project Rakshak (youth-led road-infrastructure interventions; cohorts adopt high-risk corridors). Has worked with Bengaluru Traffic Police.
- HQ: 7th Floor, SAS Tower C, Medicity, Sector 38, Gurugram (hellointern profile).
- Note on "IRSC": public materials use both "Indian Road Safety Campaign" (irsc.road-safety.co.in, framer site) and "Indian Road Safety Council" (CFI press coverage). Same founders; deck should pick one form deliberately.
